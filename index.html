Research interests

My interests lie in theoretical biophysics and physics-based approaches to computational chemistry, including molecular dynamics and quantum chemistry. I am particularly interested in fragment molecular orbital analysis of open- and closed-shell systems, alongside methods for describing strongly correlated electronic systems.

My biological interests centre on matrix metalloproteases, particularly MMP1 and the membrane-anchored MMP14, together with membrane-protein interactions and lipid bilayer modelling.

My interest in complexity theory has led me to fractal geometry, Lindenmayer systems and cellular automata. I enjoy exploring how these mathematical ideas connect with chemistry and the life sciences, particularly how local interactions generate organised behaviour across a larger system.
