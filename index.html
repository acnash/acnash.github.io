Professional background

View experience →

My career connects research at Oxford University, University College London and Imperial College London with roles in scientific start-ups. At Oxford, I combined clinical epidemiology and drug discovery to identify a potential treatment for migraine, contributing to research underpinning Human Centric Drug Discovery.

I also tutor and contribute to metalloprotease and extracellular-matrix drug discovery projects through consultancy with Arizona State University. My PhD at the University of Warwick and earlier work in artificial intelligence and software engineering underpin this combination of scientific and computational practice.
